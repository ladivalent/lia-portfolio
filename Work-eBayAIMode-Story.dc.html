eBay AI Mode

Make search better → Listen to what shoppers keep typing

How two small things shoppers typed, a size and the words “near me,” grew into an AI shopping agent that asks before it acts.

My role
Design lead
Status
Search work shipped · AI Mode is a prototype
1
The setup
eBay asked us to make search better.

A small brief: modernize how results are organized, laid out and ranked.

2
The clue
Shoppers kept typing two things search ignored.
nike air max 90 size 10 couch near me, pick up Saturday

Their size, and where they wanted it. Search treated both as noise.

3
The twist
Those weren't mistakes. People were telling us what they needed.

But “size 10” can mean a men's 10 or a women's 10. The system had to know when to guess and when to ask.

4
The fix
One simple rule for when to act.
Sure?
Do it, and show an undo.
Not sure?
Ask one question.
Can't be undone?
Wait for a yes.
5
The ending
That rule grew into AI Mode.

An AI shopping agent with 12 ways to shop and sell. It asks before it acts, and it never spends your money without your OK.

The lesson
Small things people repeat are big clues. Listen to them, then build one rule everything can follow.
