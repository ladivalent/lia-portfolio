01 · The problem
Two roles couldn't run a crew.

Owners could add only Admins or Purchasers, so crews bought freely on one account.

02 · The insight
Owners wanted a say before the purchase.

Complaints clustered in growing, multi-regional Pros, who run on approval layers. 15 interviews confirmed it.

“I don't trust my worksite employees to make job-related purchases anymore because they buy $500 worth of beef jerky on the company credit card!”
Pro account owner
03 · What I did

Three lead moves.

1
Built the umbrella

In a workshop, I made workflow the framework that three teams' features plug into.

2
Split it by role

Three phases: the owner sets rules, the requester submits, the approver decides.

3
Cut scope, not the promise

When the backend set limits, the MVP kept one spend limit and swapped edits for comments.

04 · The result

Approvals went from days to minutes.

As Pros reported after launch.

Workflow became the proof of concept for Projects: every job site rule in one place.
The lead lesson
Scope is negotiable. Control for the Pro isn't.
