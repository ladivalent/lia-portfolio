How the process evolved, 2022 → 2026

From Figma plus a ticket tracker to orchestrated agent teams with automation loops — and how the designer’s role shifted with each era.

A · Phase matrix B · Orchestrated pipeline C · Stage-gate blend Evolution 2022→26 Resilience plan
2022
Manual craft
Everything by hand, in two tools.
AI LEVERAGE~5%
THE STACK
Figma · Sketch · InVision · Miro · Zeplin · Jira
HOW IT WORKED
Every screen, spec, and deck by hand
Synthesis on sticky‑note walls
Handoff via redlines & walkthroughs
Automated: almost nothing — a few Figma plugins.
Human role: maker of screens
2023
Copilot era
Spot assistance, prompt by prompt.
AI LEVERAGE~15%
JOINED THE STACK
ChatGPT · Notion AI · FigJam AI · Copilot (eng)
WHAT CHANGED
LLMs draft copy & summaries on request
AI features land inside familiar tools
Copy‑paste between chat and canvas
Automated: first drafts of copy, notes, and brainstorms.
Human role: maker who prompts
2024
Generative era
Tools start generating the work.
AI LEVERAGE~30%
JOINED THE STACK
v0 · Vercel · Dev Mode · Dovetail AI · NotebookLM · Maze AI
WHAT CHANGED
Prompt‑to‑UI drafts in minutes
Dev Mode turns mocks into code hints
Research clustering built into tools
Automated: UI drafts, research clustering, code suggestions.
Human role: curator & editor
2025
Agent era
Agents own tasks; humans review.
AI LEVERAGE~50%
JOINED THE STACK
Claude · Cursor · MCP · Glean · CI checks
WHAT CHANGED
Agents reach tools directly via MCP
One agent owns a task end‑to‑end
Audits & spec‑diffs run on every PR
Automated: a11y audits, design QA, test synthesis, specs.
Human role: director of agents
2026+ NOW
Orchestration era
Agent teams run loops in the loop.
AI LEVERAGE~70%
JOINED THE STACK
Orchestrator + subagent teams across the stack
WHAT CHANGED
Orchestrator routes subagent teams
Loops: research → insight → backlog → QA
Human approval gates as policy
Automated: continuous loops between rituals; drafts of most artifacts.
Human role: editor‑in‑chief & approver
Making every screen
Directing agents that draft them
Synthesis weekends
Continuous research loops
Handoff decks
Spec‑and‑code contracts + QA agents
The craft didn’t change — the leverage did. A principal now runs a team of agents the way they once ran a team of juniors: clear briefs, tight feedback, human sign‑off.
